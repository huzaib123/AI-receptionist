Questions

Will it give wrong answers to my customers?

Aura only answers from the information you give us: your services, prices, hours and policies. If it doesn't know, it hands over to your WhatsApp instead of guessing.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

No. The Chatbot Only plan adds Aura to your existing site with one line of code. It works on WordPress, Wix, Shopify and custom sites.

What about patient / customer data (PDPA)?

Data is stored securely and used only for your business. We never sell or share it, and you can export or delete it at any time.

Can I change prices or services later?

Yes. Send us a WhatsApp message and we'll update it the same day at no extra cost.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month-to-month and you can cancel any time. If you leave, the website and domain are yours to keep.
